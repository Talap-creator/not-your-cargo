# Roadmap

Ordered by what blocks the next step; nothing here is promised by a date.

1. **Prove the duel across real networks** — two devices, two networks, mobile data; record three full matches (Q05/Q10).
2. **Human playtests** — six adults, C01 + three duel pairs; fix the main problem the data shows before adding content.
3. **Real token flows** — a deposit, a crate purchase and an NFT mint on mainnet, documented.
4. **Reconnect after a page reload** — a dropped WebRTC link already re-links within the room (tested 4.2 s); resuming after a reload or a new session is not implemented.
5. **Trustworthy results** — rankings are server-checked since v18; next: achievements granted by the server, then a verified NFT collection.
6. **Content** — more paints and trails, a second PvP arena, campaign difficulty tuning from real play data.
7. **Art** — pixel sprites since v18; walls, effects and UI icons still vector/code-drawn.
