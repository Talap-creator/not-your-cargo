# Steppe Raiders — audio credits

All 18 deliverables are edits of real downloaded recordings/designed assets. Every source used below is explicitly **CC0 1.0 (public-domain dedication)**. No CC-BY or unknown-license material was used. Some sci-fi/music originals are electronically designed; they are downloaded assets, not newly procedurally synthesized substitutes.

License deed: <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/> · Legal code: <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode.en>. Local copies: `sources/CC0-deed.en.html` and `sources/CC0-legalcode.en.html`.

Kenney pack pages explicitly link CC0, and their original `License.txt` files are retained. Every OpenGameArt item used explicitly lists CC0 in its License(s) field and links the CC0 deed. Saved source pages and original downloads are in `sources/`; `source_inventory.json` records download URLs, source-file hashes, and evidence paths. OGA is the publication source used for the toy mechanism; its description also links the author’s Freesound copy, but that external copy was not used.

## Edits

- **shot_mg.ogg** — First single pistol transient, high-pass/low-pass EQ, transient compression; short automatic-fire-ready tail.
- **shot_shotgun.ogg** — Layered recorded black-powder shot and cannon-like firework; compression adds body. Designed shotgun-style blast, not a recording claimed to be a shotgun.
- **shot_laser.ogg** — Downloaded designed laser, mono downmix and tail envelope.
- **shot_rocket.ogg** — Pitch-lowered downloaded thruster with a quiet recorded bang at ignition; quick launch envelope.
- **explode.ogg** — Recorded firework/cannon layered with a downloaded low-frequency explosion.
- **shot_enemy.ogg** — Different firearm recording (Magnum); darker low-pass EQ and compression.
- **hit.ogg** — Bright metal impact; compact ring-out.
- **hurt.ogg** — Heavy punch plus muffled metal hit for armored-player damage; no voice.
- **death.ogg** — Crunch explosion plus recorded firework and delayed heavy plate debris.
- **dash.ogg** — Pitch-raised thruster, high-pass EQ, rapid boost envelope.
- **pickup.ogg** — Recorded toy-shotgun cocking mechanism repurposed as a cargo latch; compressed for clarity.
- **drop.ogg** — Pitch-lowered heavy metal impact plus low punch layer.
- **deliver.ogg** — Upward steel jingle (10) for positive cargo confirmation.
- **tick.ogg** — Short band-limited computer-noise excerpt for dispatcher radio texture.
- **ui_click.ogg** — Downloaded mouse click; short, dry UI confirmation.
- **win.ogg** — Ascending steel jingle (02).
- **lose.ogg** — Descending steel jingle (14), pitched down 15%.
- **music_loop.ogg** — Desert/action source excerpt 13.333333–66.666667 s; circular 83.333 ms pre-roll crossfade from 13.250000–13.333333 s. EQ and two-pass loudnorm on three repetitions, middle period retained. No new oscillators, noise generators, or procedural composition.

All SFX: leading-silence removal, short de-click fades, 44.1 kHz mono conversion, post-Vorbis-decode peak normalization to approximately −3 dBFS. Music: 44.1 kHz stereo, −14.15 LUFS integrated, −3.09 dBFS decoded peak.

Exact ffmpeg/ffprobe commands: `COMMANDS.sh`. Rebuild: `python out/build_audio.py` from the staging root, then `python out/verify_loop.py` and `python out/write_docs.py`. Source media are never overwritten.

## Delivery-location constraint

Files are in `out/`, not the requested staging root, because the audio-editor workspace instructions require writing results to `out/`. There are no symlinks or junctions. This means the user’s root-placement acceptance condition is not met.

## Listening / integration caveat

Format, full decoding, duration, levels, source license declarations, and numerical loop-boundary continuity were checked. No human listening session or game-runtime playback was available; subjective timbre, jingle mood, absence of vocal-like material in the music, and perceptual seamlessness still need an audition in the game. The music was selected as a desert/action synth/organ/sample track; no vocal layer was added.
